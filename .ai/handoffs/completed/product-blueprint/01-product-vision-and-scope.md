# Product Vision & Scope

## Vision

Insurance Distribution Operating System (IDOS) quản lý toàn bộ hệ sinh
thái phân phối bảo hiểm.

## In Scope

-   Partner
-   Distribution
-   Sales Network
-   Integration
-   Operations
-   Governance
-   Analytics

## Out of Scope

-   Product Builder
-   Pricing Engine
-   Underwriting
-   Policy Admin
-   Claims
-   CRM
-   Commission Engine

## Core Principle

Partner-centric, Capability Ownership, Adaptive UI.
