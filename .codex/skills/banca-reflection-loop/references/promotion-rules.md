# Lesson promotion rules

| State | Evidence |
|---|---|
| OBSERVED | One recorded occurrence |
| CANDIDATE | Two matching fingerprints or strong causal evidence |
| VALIDATED | Prevention succeeded and regression passed |
| APPROVED | Reviewed safe operating rule |
| RETIRED | Superseded or ineffective |

Technical safety rules may be proposed automatically. Business, legal, permission, state, terminology, and UX-architecture lessons require explicit review before approval.
